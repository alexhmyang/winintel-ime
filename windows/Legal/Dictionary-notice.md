# Dictionary data

The WIME0002 index is generated by winintel AI from GPL-3.0 dictionary data
at https://github.com/iDvel/rime-ice, revision
927e1cf1d3eeb9bf4be85e3f684dba4dd485b6f6, plus winintel starter terms.

The Chinese sources are 8105, 41448, base, ext, tencent and others;
the English sources are en and en_ext. Source blob identifiers are recorded
in dictionary-provenance.json. The GPL license is included with the product.
This incorporates word data into the independent winintel engine.
